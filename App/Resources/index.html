About you

Optional. It helps the coach give advice that fits you. It stays on this device and is only sent to Claude with your questions if you add an API key. Your weight comes from your weigh-ins.

Claude AI

Paste your API key once. It is saved on this phone only, is never put in your backup text, and is sent only to Anthropic when you ask a question.

Prices are per million tokens. Opus is the default. Fable is the smartest and costs twice as much. Sonnet and Haiku are cheaper. The time Claude spends thinking is billed as output, so Deep costs more than Quick. A typical question costs 2 to 10 cents on Opus with Deep thinking.

Monthly credit

The app counts every request it sends, using the token counts Anthropic returns and its published prices. It cannot see spending from anywhere else you use this key. If the number ever differs from the Console, type the Console’s balance into “Credit left”.
